This is the most frequently asked question by everyone entering the investment market. Once you master this logic, you can see through the cruelty of inflation and the terrifying power of compound interest in a single second. </p> <h2 class="text-2xl font-serif font-bold mt-10 mb-4  pl-3">I. What is the Rule of 72?</h2> <p> The Rule of 72 is a mathematical formula used to quickly calculate the "time required to double the principal."<br> The formula is very simple: <strong>72 ÷ Annualized Rate of Return = Number of years required to double the principal.</strong><br> You don't need a complex scientific calculator; you can get the answer directly in your head. The core of this formula is based on the compounding effect, which Einstein called the "eighth wonder of the world." </p> <h2 class="text-2xl font-serif font-bold mt-10 mb-4  pl-3">II. Cruel Comparison: Fixed Deposits vs. Market ETFs</h2> <p>Let's use the Rule of 72 to look at the real power of different financial tools:</p> <ul class="list-disc pl-6 space-y-4 mb-6"> <li> <strong>Bank Fixed Deposit (Assuming 2% annual interest rate):</strong> 72 ÷ 2 = 36 years. This means if you put 1 million in the bank, you have to wait exactly 36 years for it to become 2 million. In the high-inflation environment of 2026, the purchasing power of that 2 million after 36 years will have long been eaten away by inflation. </li> <li> <strong>Steady Market ETF (Assuming 7% annualized return):</strong> 72 ÷ 7 ≈ 10.2 years. The same 1 million, just placed in a market-cap ETF that tracks economic growth, can double to 2 million in about 10 years. If you have an investment horizon of 30 years, your assets will experience a geometric explosion of "doubling 3 times" (1 million ➡️ 2 million ➡️ 4 million ➡️ 8 million). </li> </ul> <h2 class="text-2xl font-serif font-bold mt-10 mb-4  pl-3">III.
